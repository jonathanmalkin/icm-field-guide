# NLP Logix

- **Pattern:** Acquisition and relationship flywheel
- **Forms:** Umbrella of training, delivery, content, and partnership Pipelines
  linked to an authoritative CRM
- **Repeating unit:** an opportunity or partnership workstream
- **ICM application:** business diagnosis → opportunity brief → engagement →
  build/case study/content → next opportunity.
- **Boundary:** contracts, relationship state, tasks, and revenue facts belong in
  their authoritative business systems.
- **Evidence:** source-claimed partnership and commercial model; figures are unverified.
