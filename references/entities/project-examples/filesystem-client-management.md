# Filesystem client management

- **Pattern:** CRM operations plus Client Delivery
- **Forms:** client Record Library + business-development Pipeline
- **Repeating unit:** a lead, client, or engagement
- **ICM application:** business-dev area, client template, proposals,
  communications, deliverables, and case studies.
- **Boundary:** suitable as a one-operator starter; when a CRM exists, the CRM
  owns contacts, opportunity stages, activities, and tasks.
- **Evidence:** captured Vault workflow template.
