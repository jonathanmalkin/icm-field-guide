# Clief Notes AI librarian

- **Patterns:** Research and knowledge hub + Training and workforce enablement
- **Repeating unit:** a learner question or navigation decision
- **ICM application:** route a learner to an existing lesson, source, or next
  action inside a bounded educational corpus.
- **Boundary:** the classroom and feed remain authoritative. The agent is a
  navigator, not a new curriculum, outcome guarantee, access-control system,
  or source of private member information.
- **Evidence:** source-described community platform behavior; not independently
  operated or verified by this Field Guide.
