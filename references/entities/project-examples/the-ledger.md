# The Ledger

- **Pattern:** marketplace/relationship system; closest to CRM operations plus a
  talent Record Library
- **Forms:** candidate and company Record Libraries + matching/placement Pipelines
- **Repeating unit:** a candidate, hiring company, role, or placement
- **ICM application:** portfolio evidence and capability context may be plain
  files; identity, consent, applications, commercial state, and matching events
  require a real platform or CRM.
- **Evidence:** source-described launch/product claims; implementation was not
  source inspected.
