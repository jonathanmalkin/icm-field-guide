# Method factory and instances

- **Composition:** Knowledge Bundle → configurable Pipeline instances, often
  under an Umbrella
- **Use for:** productized expertise, software-in-service, reusable consulting
  methods, and licensed workflows.
- **Factory:** sanitized method, decision rules, examples, templates, and quality bar.
- **Instance:** buyer/client configuration, inputs, outputs, and local evidence.
- **Flow:** configure → execute → review → deliver → propose sanitized learning.
- **Human check:** approve both delivery and any promotion back into the factory.
- **Boundary:** instances never share client facts.
