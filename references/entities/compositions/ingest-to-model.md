# Ingest to model

- **Composition:** Pipeline → Knowledge Bundle
- **Use for:** voice capture, conversations, transcripts, research ingestion,
  session knowledge, or observed-workflow extraction.
- **Flow:** raw capture → normalize/transcribe → extract candidate claims or
  patterns → human promotion → navigable bundle.
- **State:** raw corpus manifest plus stage outputs; the bundle is the reviewed product.
- **Human check:** promotion into durable knowledge.
- **Remove:** extraction stages that do not improve provenance, routing, or quality.
