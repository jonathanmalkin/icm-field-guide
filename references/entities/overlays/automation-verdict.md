# Automation verdict

- **Trigger:** someone proposes automating or agentifying a workflow.
- **Verdicts:** keep manual; use ordinary files/checklist/prompt; build a
  human-gated ICM; build deterministic software or orchestration.
- **Assess:** repetition, judgment load, input variability, failure cost,
  reversibility, volume, and the desired outcome.
- **Risk check:** money, reputation, irreversible action, regulated or sensitive material.
- **Human check:** the owner accepts the verdict and the remaining manual gates.
- **Removal test:** choose the lowest-effort mechanism that meets the quality
  and safety bar.
- **Role models:** Workflow Audit, Effort-to-Output Ladder, Tool Ladder, 60/30/10.
