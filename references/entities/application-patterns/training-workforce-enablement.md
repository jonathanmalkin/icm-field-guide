# Training and workforce enablement

Training and workforce enablement ICM makes a cohort, workshop, session, or
learner implementation both a learning event and a structured source of
evidence about real work.

## Use it when

Use a [Pipeline](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/e5313c308522ff9c4646a17b06fb1ddb0d844917/references/forms.md#1-pipeline--the-production-line)
to prepare, teach, exercise, capture, and review. Use a
[Record Library](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/e5313c308522ff9c4646a17b06fb1ddb0d844917/references/forms.md#3-record-library--the-unit-is-a-record)
when cohorts, sessions, or implementations accumulate and are compared over
time.

## What it contains

- stable curriculum, facilitation rules, and exercises;
- a cohort or session record;
- participant-created working artifacts;
- reviewed findings about workflows, capability, or friction;
- an opportunity map or enablement plan;
- an optional handoff into a build specification.

The flow is: prepare → teach → exercise → capture structured artifacts → review
→ enable the learner or specify the next build.

## Human boundary

Facilitators validate conclusions before they become build priorities,
performance claims, or published examples. Participant and client information
remains isolated. Remove learner tracking when no recurring program consumes
it.
