# Creative artifact production

Creative artifact production turns an approved intent into an animation,
design, video, interactive, or campaign asset. Its defining move is a human-
editable creative brief or specification before expensive construction.

## Use it when

Use a [Pipeline](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/e5313c308522ff9c4646a17b06fb1ddb0d844917/references/forms.md#1-pipeline--the-production-line)
when one medium follows a repeatable direction-to-output sequence. Use an
[Umbrella](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/e5313c308522ff9c4646a17b06fb1ddb0d844917/references/forms.md#2-umbrella--a-portfolio-of-pipelines)
when several media pipelines share style, components, or brand references.

## What it contains

- approved direction and source content;
- a creative brief or specification describing intent and constraints;
- stable style, component, and technical references;
- build and render or export stages;
- review artifacts that compare the result with the approved brief.

The flow is: direction → script or content → creative specification → build →
render or export → review.

## Human boundary

Approve the specification before technical construction. Preserve room for
creative judgment: avoid pixel-level or code-level instructions unless they are
true constraints. Merge content and build when the medium is simple enough that
the extra handoff does not reduce rework.
