# Team or company brain

Use this orientation when people cannot readily trace ownership, handoffs,
inputs, outputs, or the authoritative system for a process. The repeating unit
is an organization node: a team, process, data asset, governance rule, or
handoff.

The likely canonical orientation is a
[Context Map](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/b20fb45063a564cf607b03526e206f519d174def/references/forms.md#5-context-map--the-organization-as-a-graph).
That canonical reference—not this card—defines its structure and contracts.
Use a [Knowledge Bundle](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/b20fb45063a564cf607b03526e206f519d174def/references/forms.md#4-knowledge-bundle--the-product-is-the-knowledge)
only when people must navigate deeper policies, concepts, or evidence.

Owners validate the map and choose any pilot. The map links to CRM, ERP,
accounting, trackers, drives, and databases; it never replaces their live
truth. Start with [system archetypes](../system-archetypes/README.md) if the
decision is fundamentally CRM- or ERP-shaped.
