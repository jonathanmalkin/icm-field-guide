# Content production

Content production ICM turns one approved idea into one reviewed, publishable
artifact while reusing stable voice, format, evidence, and brand constraints.

## Use it when

Use a [Pipeline](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/b20fb45063a564cf607b03526e206f519d174def/references/forms.md#1-pipeline--the-production-line)
when research, drafting, production, and review repeat with real human pauses.
Use an [Umbrella](https://github.com/RinDig/icm-architect/blob/b20fb45063a564cf607b03526e206f519d174def/references/forms.md#2-umbrella--a-portfolio-of-pipelines)
when distinct formats such as articles, video, and social posts share one
factory but follow different production lines.

## What it contains

- stable voice, format, brand, and evidence references;
- an approved topic or direction artifact;
- only the stages where a person can cheaply correct downstream work;
- editable research, outline, draft, and production artifacts as needed;
- a final review and publication gate.

The common flow is: approve direction → gather evidence → shape the argument →
draft → produce the channel-specific artifact → approve publication.

## Human boundary

A person owns topic choice, claims, taste, and publication. Merge any stages
whose separation does not reduce rework or improve a decision. A short,
low-risk artifact may need only a saved skill rather than a workspace.
